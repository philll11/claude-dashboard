## Plan limits

Claude Code passes your plan limits to its status line command while it runs in a terminal.
Connected, claude-dashboard becomes that command: it records each reading, then shows its own line or the one you had.

- The dashboard shows the change to Claude Code's `settings.json` before making it, and keeps a backup.
- **Disconnect** in the dashboard's Limits section puts your own status line back.
- The status line runs a copy of claude-dashboard kept with your history, so updates to the extension never break it.

The status bar then shows the five-hour window and when it resets, and the sidebar lists every window.
